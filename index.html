03 About Me

I am passionate about design and focused on delivery. I have had over 10 years experience working as a UI Designer & Product Designer at ASOS and other startups where I transform initial thoughts and ideas to fully functional features that are engaged by millions of customers worldwide. Customer centric experiences are of top priority across 3 major platforms. I have an interest in Ai and frontend development creating actual working interactive experiences and am keen to develop further into this domain.

Hobbies & Interests

With 20+ years of experience using Adobe Creative Suite, I enjoy keeping my skills current, particularly in Photoshop and Illustrator, and exploring new creative tools and techniques. In my spare time, I enjoy drawing, primarily using Procreate. I'm also an avid collector of pens and have recently been experimenting with acrylic markers and gouache paints.

Watercolour painting is another creative passion of mine, with subjects ranging from silhouettes and fantasy themes to typography. I also create handmade watercolour paints, specialising in artisan metallic and neon colours, which I occasionally sell through my Etsy shop. Alongside painting, I enjoy paper crafts, vinyl cutting and printmaking. I love designing and creating personalised stationery and gifts using foiling techniques. Having taught myself both foiling and digital cutting, I enjoy combining metallic finishes with handcrafted designs to create unique pieces.

Regular exercise is important to me, both physically and mentally. The gym helps me stay active after long days at a desk and provides a great opportunity to socialise with friends.
